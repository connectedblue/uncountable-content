<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s been five months now since my <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2026/06/biological-recording-training/" data-type="post" data-id="7299">biological recording training</a> with the <a href="https://www.gloucestershirewildlifetrust.co.uk/volunteer">Gloucestershire Wildlife Trust</a>, and today was the first time I&#8217;ve managed to get out with the group to record.  Pete invited us to a tree walk along the canal by the ocean, where we&#8217;d learn some tree ID techniques and record some of what we saw into iRecord.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We then ambled pretty slowly towards Bond&#8217;s Mill, stopping by different species to spot characteristics and take photographs.  There was a hedgerow half way along which I helped to plant with <a href="https://www.stroudvalleysproject.org/">Stroud Valleys Project</a> a few years ago which is now standing pretty tall and robust.  It&#8217;s mostly hawthorn, but there is also a good variety of spindle, hazel and other species.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s a pretty good time of year to look closely at trees because they still have their fruits and berries.  They also mostly had their leaves, not showing any sign of turning autumn colours.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the way back we then recorded various items we&#8217;d seen directly into the app.  Hopefully between us we have recorded some good examples of the tree diversity along that stretch.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the afternoon, I headed into Dursley to start distributing some <a href="https://discoverdursley.org.uk/thrivelocal/">Thrive Local</a> guides and cards into different venues.  This is a project I&#8217;ve worked on for the past few months and it&#8217;s time now to launch to the community.  I dropped off a stock at the town council and library.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Finally, I headed back to the library to run the second of this week&#8217;s <a href="https://www.facebook.com/dursleycodeclub">Dursley Code Club</a>.  Six children today doing various projects and I was joined by Martha, a computer science student from Rednock who wants to volunteer with the club.</p>
