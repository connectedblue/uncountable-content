<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There was still an awful lot in flower, so a lot to see in all the different areas.  As well as formal beds, there were quite a lot of mini orchards dotted about.  The conservatory and orangery areas were also interesting with yew clipped into all sorts of shapes.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But the highlight was the walled garden, some way from the house.  As well as a huge greenhouse and flower bed, there were large vegetable patches and more fruit trees and orchards. All in all, we spent about 3.5 hours there.</p>
