<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An early start today, travelling to a remote site in the North Cotswolds with <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a>.  A bit of a mix up in the morning, as the task and location got changed at 8am when I already had left.  I was stood waiting at the orginal location wondering where everyone was, when all of a sudden Robina drove past and spotted me.  That was a stroke of luck since she knew where we should have been.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The task today was to clear gorse from a steep, species rich grassland slope.  It was tough work.  We weren&#8217;t working too far down the slope, but even so, dragging the arisings uphill was tough work.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I spent pretty much the whole session on dragging duty, along with Chris.  We had tarpaulins, which is the only practical way of moving gorse, and every time we managed to get one to the pile at the top, another tarp was loaded up by the team.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By the end of the afternoon, we had amassed a huge pile of gorse on the flat field at the top of the slope.  The farmer plans to take it over to another field to be burnt, so a good result all round.</p>
