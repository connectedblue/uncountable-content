<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you live in the UK, did you buy petrol last week?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If so, then thank you. A very small fraction of your payment accrued directly to me. I own a small piece of the oil company that either traded, supplied or drilled the fuel (or all three). If you purchased from a supermarket, then an additional fraction from the retail sale would also flow through to me. And if you happened to buy a chocolate bar as well, there&#8217;s a very good chance that a further fraction also came directly to me.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Of course, there was nothing special about you, or the place you bought the fuel that day.  The customer before and after you also sent a fraction of their payment my way.  As did every other customer that moment in all the fuel outlets around the country.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All the companies involved in that transaction are both publicly listed and dividend paying.  I own a small piece of every major company in the UK and US through index funds.  There&#8217;s a direct line between the dividend payment I receive at the end of the financial year and the payment you made last week.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Of course, the amount coming to me from your transaction is very small, probably less than 0.000000001p.  But, crucially, it&#8217;s not zero.  It&#8217;s a positive number.  And if you add up enough positive numbers, then eventually you get a large one.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I&#8217;m a practising capitalist, but also a lazy one.  I&#8217;ve been invested in large public companies for decades through regular contributions to index tracker funds.  You don&#8217;t have to be clever, or insightful, or really any skill at all.  You just have to show up to the market with money and keep it there.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To people who don&#8217;t intentionally invest, the stock market probably seems like a mystery.  But it doesn&#8217;t have to be.  You can buy the whole market in one transaction, and then start benefiting from those fractional payments that occur continuously throughout the economy 24/7.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Investing is straightforward.</p>
