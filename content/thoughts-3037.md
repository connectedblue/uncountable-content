<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I never really ever thought too much about purpose during my career.  I suppose I was more more goal oriented, and to the extent I thought about it at all, I probably would have said my purpose was to pursue my goals.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Since stopping work in 2019, I have been acutely aware of <a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/replacing-work-benefits/" data-type="post" data-id="413">structure, identity and purpose</a> and take pro-active steps to ensure my lifestyle delivers on all three pillars.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After the initial decompression that happens after stopping work, you begin to settle in a routine which is very different to the one you led for the last few decades.  One stand out characteristic is that, for the first time in your life, no-one demands anything of you.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is a jarring feeling.  To paraphrase Oscar Wilde, there&#8217;s only one thing worse than being pulled in all directions, and that&#8217;s not being pulled in any direction.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At first you relish this state of being.  It doesn&#8217;t mean you are being ignored, or bored or lonely.  You can go about your business doing whatever you please.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But after a while, you may get a peculiar urge to have someone actually demand something of you.  So you can feel relevant in their life.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">That is purpose.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Today, I would describe my purpose as helping to improve the community and environment around me.  Although it sounds kinda vague and wishy-washy, it&#8217;s incredibly helpful in whittling down activities that I do.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Some of the projects I have underway are running an after school code club in the library for 8-13 year olds, restoring wildflower meadows in the Cotswolds, running a website for my local town, creating a wetland nature reserve, monitoring river health through riverfly and running a monthly Green Drinks meeting for people interested in similar things.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Aside from the coding type things, none of these activities were even remotely on my radar while I was working.  So, I don&#8217;t really believe that purpose has to be singular, unchanging north star.  Instead it&#8217;s just a impulsive responsive to wanting to feel wanted.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In other words, your purpose is to have a purpose.</p>
