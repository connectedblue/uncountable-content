<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I was <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2026/01/browns-piece/" data-type="post" data-id="6858">last over at Brown&#8217;s Piece</a> in January, and today <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a> returned to help with raking the meadow prior to seeding. This community owned asset was purchased in September 2025, and they had flailed the entire meadow, getting rid of the thatch prior to the seeding.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The whole site looked very good &#8211; the circular scrub areas we cut back earlier in the year were contained and the grass had restored nicely around them.  The bank I cut today I had also done back in January.  Not a lot of bramble had encroached, so it was relatively easy to get that back down to the level of the rest of the meadow.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I had to leave around lunch time in order to get back to run the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/dursleycodeclub">Dursley Code Club</a> in the library.  The Monday club had  taken a little while to get going this term, but today we had seven.  Some good creative work by Jack to solve a way to make his scratch game scroll.</p>
