<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Purpose</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A single word that evokes many different interpretations.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It can apply to yourself, an organisation, club, community or even humanity itself.  Is it fixed for all time, or does it change and adapt to the surrounding environment?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Is it important for others to know the purpose, or can it just be shared among the people with a direct stake or influence on it?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Why even have a purpose defined at all?  Would anything be different if it wasn&#8217;t?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Do wildlife have a purpose, beyond the biological processes that drive particular behaviours in each moment?  Or, is it a uniquely human construct to separate us from other life on earth?</p>





<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Let&#8217;s hear your perspectives on Purpose through this month!</p>
