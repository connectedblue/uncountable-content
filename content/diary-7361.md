<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An altogether different harvest day, starting with being picked up by Chris in Gloucester.  Today&#8217;s event was organised only recently and I already had the car booked in for a repair.  But Chris very kindly picked me up from the garage and we headed over to Bannerdown Common near Bath.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In addition to the normal harvest, we were holding an event for the South Cotswold farm cluster where the members could see how the process works and ask questions.  They were due to show later in the morning so we had a bit of time to set up and start.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The cluster folk turned up around 11.30, so we stopped and Anna gave them a talk about the project and the process.  There was then a demo of the harvesting process by Tom and lots of questions about how we dried, weighed and distributed seed.  There was quite the crowd, over 15 people.  This also included members of the public, including Susan and David who are my fellow volunteers at Fromebridge.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Overall, it was a very informative and useful event.  Anna and Tom picked up lots of contact details during the general mingling so hopefully that will lead to some business down the line.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">They disappeared off for a lunch somewhere and we were then able to crack on with finishing the field.  Overall, I&#8217;d say we collected somewhere around 40kg which wasn&#8217;t too bad considering we lost of couple of hours for the event.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Chris then dropped me off back at Gloucester and picked up my repaired car.  In the end this was much better and productive day that hanging round Gloucester docks would have been. </p>
