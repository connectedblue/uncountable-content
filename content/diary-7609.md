<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There&#8217;s been a lot of change up at Stinchcombe this year, with fencing and grazing introduced on the SSSI. But oddly enough, I hadn&#8217;t actually walked that part of the common all year.  Today was a good opportunity to drive over to <a href="https://leafandground.com/">Leaf &amp; Ground</a> and walk a nice circular to look at progress.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I must admit, I was bowled over to the improvement in the grassland already.  The steep slopes had been cut and raked (I guess for the final time) and although I didn&#8217;t see them, the cattle have been doing their thing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A couple of years back I helped with some baseline botanical surveys of the area, and I look forward to hopefully doing some again next season to start to record progress.  The most impressive part was just past the Tubbs bench where trees had been cut down a couple of years ago and grassland was very much returning.  While there were small tree shoots and brambles poking through, you&#8217;d hope that the hardy cattle breeds would chomp through and keep that down.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s been a huge effort by many volunteers over many years to get to this point, but it really feels like the area has reached a turning point and on course to becoming a vast species rich grassland in years to come.</p>
