<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is my 1,000th blog post!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Seems quite a milestone, so today&#8217;s post (6 July 2026) will be dedicated to my writing journey.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I blog under the domain <code><a href="https://uncountable.uk/">uncountable.uk</a></code>.  That&#8217;s a sprawling, unconnected mess of stuff (for example, my <a href="https://apps.uncountable.uk/">app store</a> floats somewhere there).  But the main writing lives under <code><a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/">diary.uncountable.uk</a></code> and <code><a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/">thoughts.uncountable.uk</a></code>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I started making regular diary entries in 2023, although I also <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/series/past-days/">recall days from the past</a>.   If you&#8217;d like to start a blog and don&#8217;t know what to blog about, then I&#8217;d definitely recommend a diary format.  The content writes itself, and people enjoy reading about other people&#8217;s lives.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The diary format would quickly become confusing though if you suddenly want to write about <a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/everything-is-less-than-0-12/">how everything is less than 0.12</a>. So in 2024 I started a separate space called Uncountable Thoughts which is text only, minimally formatted, and flexible enough to write about whatever I want.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Two interesting moments of writing history are also preserved.  In 2011, I went on a trip to New Zealand and wrote a blog.  I stumbled upon it again recently and <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/series/new-zealand-holiday/">imported it into my diary</a>.  And in 2012, I launched a brief blogging career called Just My Thoughts Aloud.  It lasted all of 6 posts, but they are also <a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/topic/first-blog/">imported here for posterity</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So, without further ado, here&#8217;s how the 1,000 posts break down:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Contemporaneous <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/">diary entries</a>:  651</li>



<li>Recollections of past days:  23</li>



<li>Articles <a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/thoughts-on/money/" data-type="category" data-id="7">about money</a>:  53</li>



<li>Interviews with <a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/topic/people-and-money/" data-type="post_tag" data-id="67">people about money</a>:  14</li>



<li><a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/thoughts-on/reply-posts/" data-type="category" data-id="61">Reply posts</a>:  47</li>



<li><a href="https://thoughts.uncountable.uk/thoughts-on/digital-life/" data-type="category" data-id="6">Digital living</a>:  132</li>



<li>Random topics:  80</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A blog can be whatever you want it to be.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">My next 1,000 posts might come quite quickly. I have 700 or so exported posts from LinkedIn written between 2018 and 2022. These aren&#8217;t blogs in the traditional sense, but I feel they represent my writing and should be homed here, especially since freezing my LinkedIn.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I didn&#8217;t really set out to be a blogger.  I was a late starter at 55, and just needed to somehow find a way to document all the many things I had started doing since stopping work.  But it was definitely the best digital project I&#8217;ve done, and I fully intend to keep typing as long as I am able.</p>
