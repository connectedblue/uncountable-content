<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I picked up Georgie early this morning and we headed up to the Friendship Cafe in Gloucester for an event by <a href="https://www.creategloucestershire.co.uk/">Create Gloucestershire</a>.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The topic was how to get creativity into communities in Gloucestershire, and the format was an Open Space.  This means the agenda is decided by the people in the room.  People who wanted to start a conversation on something would put an agenda item under a session and claim a space to hold the discussion.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I submitted a topic for each session, but no-one came to my first one on digital creativity.  That gave me an opportunity to attend a couple of other sessions on activities for under 12&#8217;s and another on what the community wants.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Next up was an absolutely delicious lunch from the <a href="https://thefriendshipcafe.com/">Friendship Cafe</a>.  Vegetable biriyani  and somosas with yoghurt dressing.  Huge pots to serve from, and plenty to take home later.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the second session, I lead a discussion about venues for creativity.  This time I had four people join and we had a good hour&#8217;s discussion on the topic.   Abi from the Quarry Chapel joined us and we had an excellent catch up, which we haven&#8217;t had the opportunity to do back in Dursley.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the evening we had <a href="https://dursleygreen.org.uk/dursley-green-drinks/">Dursley Green Drinks</a>.  Jo wasn&#8217;t able to attend this one so I&#8217;d be running on my own.  A really good attendance of about 13-14, including three new faces.</p>
