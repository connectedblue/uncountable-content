<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There&#8217;s been a lot of chatter recently about the future of <a href="https://www.kingshillhouse.org.uk/">Kingshill House</a> and whether Stroud District council will continue the funding.  So today was an opportunity to throw open the doors to everyone and showcase what activities take place there.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I&#8217;ve been there many times over the last few years, but there&#8217;s always new things to see and learn.  They had a very comprehensive history section going through all the ownership and uses over the years.  We also had a tour of the cottage out the back which I&#8217;ve not been in before.  It was rented out until quite recently, but is in need of some major work before it can be again.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There was a performance of music upstairs and some craft activities where I pressed my painted hand into a cloth with other supporters of the house.  There was also another room I&#8217;d never been in before where the glass blowing workshops are held.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There&#8217;ll be a drive to raise funds to keep the house under community ownership, regardless of what SDC finally decide.  A big show of support today, but a long road ahead.</p>
