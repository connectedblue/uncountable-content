<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I can&#8217;t quite believe this is my first proper balsam bashing session of the year with <a href="https://www.stroudvalleysproject.org/">Stroud Valleys Project</a>.  The plants are now quite tall and in flower, so a critical time to start pulling.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We met in the Eastington Industrial Estate, an area I <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2025/05/first-balsam-pull/" data-type="post" data-id="6086">did last year</a>.  It was good to see the first section of the river had very little &#8211; we spent a lot of time on these banks last year so that seemed to work well.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We then made our way up the Oldbury brook towards the new lock where we didn&#8217;t have time to complete properly last year.  There were quite a few of us, so we managed to split up methodically, with some in the water and others on the bank.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We got permission to cross the work site and then we could work on the other side of the brook bank.  By around 2pm we had finished a pretty thorough job &#8211; both sides of the brook cleared all the way down to the Frome.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Will definitely be interesting to revisit this section next year and see how much comes back.</p>
