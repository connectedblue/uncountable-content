<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Met up early with Graham and Maran to do our regular monthly riverfly check on the Frome site.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The levels are still accessible, although it&#8217;s quite a bit higher than it has been recently.  I&#8217;ve noted in previous months recently that Mayfly are still appearing , and today was no exception with 15 spotted.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Not a large number of species, but most of them had a score of 2, which meant we were seeing relatively healthy numbers of each one.  Overall the score was 8, which is pretty good for this site.</p>
