<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The particularly prevalent flower was wild marjoram which could be clearly smelt in a scented waft as the seeds were sifted.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The location was next to Hailey Wood and the grassland sat nicely between two woodland edges with no scrub edge at all. One very nice feature was a single standard spindle tree next to an oak. Not something you see very often. Tom thought it might actually be around 100 years old despite it&#8217;s small size.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We gathered a ton of seed.  It was ankle deep across the whole tarpaulin by the time we came to bag up at the end of the day.</p>
