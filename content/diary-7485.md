<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tony has been organising today&#8217;s event, <a href="https://www.heritagehub.org.uk/events/go-with-the-flow/">Go with the Flow</a>, for over a year now. It was up in the heritage hub at Gloucester, which is a building I haven&#8217;t visited before.  The format was an exhibition and series of talks on river heritage from various organisations around Gloucestershire.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The model boat exhibits were fantastic.  There&#8217;s a large club of enthusiasts who build the powered boats to scale, and then they do sailings in various locations around the county.  There are discussions underway with the Stroudwater Canal project &#8211; it would be great to see them at Fromebridge.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There was also an excellent audio-visual exhibit which was a collection of heritage research projects by school students. The exhibit consisted of slides which the students narrated over. This was played on a loop. It&#8217;s such an excellent method of engaging and presenting work and it could be applied to almost any topic. A couple of the stand out talks were on the Gloucester floods of 1947 and the Purton hulks.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I caught the end of the talks in the other room, and mingled during the break.  It was a very well attended event and Tony was pleased with the outcome.  I spoke to Lois about putting the Fromebridge parts of my diary into the archives, which we&#8217;d talked about before a while back.  It should be pretty straightforward &#8211; I&#8217;ll just have to generate the relevant pages into PDFs for each year.  Another project for the winter months ahead. </p>
