<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A long journey this morning to the eastern edge of the Cotswolds with <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a> to a major new site for the project.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s in an extremely remote, and beautiful corner, so I was mightily surprised to see many cars in the field and people on the road. Turns out this was the location of <a href="https://www.wiltsglosstandard.co.uk/news/26407349.rare-long-tailed-shrike-spotted-cotswold-farm/">a single long-tailed shrike</a> blown off course from Kazakhstan and twitchers from all over have descended on the farm to take a look.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The initial location by the field gate was extremely windy and exposed, so we hopped in Tom&#8217;s truck and drove to a nice sheltered spot in a dip on the far side, next to a woodland.  This proved to be a good choice with a nice flat area to lay out the tarp.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We were visited by Sam, the tenant farmer, who gave us an overview of his project called the Cotswold Savannah &#8211; a bold vision to create 5,000 acres of species rich grazing land for native cattle breeds, and possibly, in the longer term, bison.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The field we were harvesting was arable 30 years ago and since then has not been overseeded &#8211; everything in here has arrived naturally.  So he wants to extend this vision across the entire site he manages (his tenancy plus an adjoining estate).  It will take many years and decades to come to full fruition, but the intention is to start now as a reaction to the financial challenges of organic farming.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This year&#8217;s seed will be stored for redistribution in recipient sites next year as the first phase of this grand project.  Sam is certainly very passionate about what he wants to do, and it&#8217;s a good motivator for our project.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By 3pm we had collected six large bags and avoided the rain, so all in all a pretty productive day.  </p>
