<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I had done this about three years ago back to the fence, but it had encroached quite a bit.  I couldn&#8217;t really see the fence, but there was grass growing underneath the bramble so definitely worth knocking back.  About half way along was a large willow that also protruded right into the grassland, so I enlisted the help of Rene to cut that back.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Delicious chocolate cake from Louise at the mid morning break.   By now it had got surprisingly hot in the open field.  I had an appointment in Dursley in the afternoon, so I left around quarter past twelve.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the afternoon I met with Leah at Jacob&#8217;s House to set up some email configuration to support the forthcoming Thrive Local event guide release.  We&#8217;ll be doing a promotion tomorrow at the Saturday market so we discussed that a bit as well.</p>
