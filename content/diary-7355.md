<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A change of plan today with <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a>.  Tom called me yesterday to tell me his harvester had broken, so not everyone was needed to collect today since we were down to one.  So instead I went to the barn to help get the spreadsheet in order to track this year&#8217;s haul.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We did a similar exercise last year, but ended up with quite a complicated spreadsheet which tracked individual bags of seed.  This was really the wrong approach &#8211; the key thing that needs tracking is the overall weight of seed collected from sites, and how that gets allocated to the demand from recipient sites.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So, I started with a very simple toy example where we built a model based on 3 donors, 4 recipients and 2 mixes (a mix is a combination of donor sites to create a new blend before it&#8217;s allocated to a recipient).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I used the principles of double entry book keeping, so the transaction ledger simultaneously decrements one account balance and increases another.  This made it possible to track movements of how recipient orders are fulfilled.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It seemed to work well, so while Tom went off in the afternoon to help Cerian with a flat trailer tyre, I stayed in the barn to implement the system.  By the time he returned, I had it sketched out and we tested it by entering all the data to date.  A pivot table then gave a comprehensive snapshot overview of where we currently are.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hopefully this will lead to a more straightforward allocation and tracking process later in the season.</p>
