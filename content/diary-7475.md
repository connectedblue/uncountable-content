<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Met with Graham at 9am to do our regular riverfly check near the Ocean.  Graham had added a new piece of equipment &#8211; a small portable table to examine the samples on instead of kneeling on the ground.  This is a game changer, especially in the winter months.  It&#8217;s a bit of extra bulk to carry the 15 minutes or so to the river, but well worth it.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I took the sample, and the river level didn&#8217;t seem very much higher despite the small bit of rain we&#8217;d had in the previous days.  But we were rewarded straight away with an unusual sight &#8211; a Mayfly at this time of year.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I haven&#8217;t seen one since ... well May probably.  But it turned out to not be a one-off &#8211; there were 14 in the sample.  And plenty of other species too, including a caseless caddis.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Overall a score of 9, which is a big boost from the slim pickings we&#8217;ve had at that site over the summer.  Maybe an early indication that the river habitat is starting to recover from the drought conditions we&#8217;ve been experiencing in the last few months.</p>
