If they enjoy the activity, there&#8217;s an opportunity to have formal training in January.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The weather had been very rainy the last few days, and the immediate problem was the high river levels at the railway bridge where the session was being run.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s a regular site for Barry, so he elected to go in carefully and gather the sample.  We&#8217;d normally allow new people to try this part, but the river was too fast flowing for that.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We did however get a pretty good sample and set up two trays for everyone to take a look.  There were quite a few species &#8211; certainly compared to what Graham and I experience at our nearby regular site.  We went over to look at it, and it was pretty full &#8211; we probably won&#8217;t get a chance to go in for the remainder  of this year.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Overall pretty successful morning &#8211; we got more new people interested in the activity, so hopefully they&#8217;ll be joining our regular groups soon.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the afternoon, I headed over to the library for the second <a href="https://www.facebook.com/dursleycodeclub">Dursley Code Club</a> of the week.  Another full house today &#8211; new people coming along and some swaps between the Monday and Tuesday groups.</p>
