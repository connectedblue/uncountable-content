<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Today the <a href="https://gloslibraries.uk/lab">Innovation Lab from Gloucestershire libraries</a> visited Dursley to do a pop up lab session and share some of their activities.  I had already briefed my <a href="https://www.facebook.com/dursleycodeclub">Dursley Code Club</a> members about the day, but was also hoping to attract some new ones who haven&#8217;t heard about us.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Andy and Steve from the Lab ran the sessions.  They had a number of activities available from robot programming, music making to graphics tablets and a cardboard shape cutter.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Right from the off at 10am we had 4 children show up, so I knew we&#8217;d be in for a busy day.  The session was very informal, just drop in and out whenever and take part in whatever you wish.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By 11am we had a steady stream &#8211; at one point there were 9 around the tables.  There were quite a few who were new to me, so it was a good opportunity to talk to parents about the after school code club I run, and get them on my mailing list.  There&#8217;s a few vacancies next term, so hopefully they&#8217;ll get filled soon.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By the end of the session we had 33 children visit, 15-20 parents and 8 signed up to the code club newsletter.  So very satisfied with the outcome, and I&#8217;ll be looking to see if we can repeat this event every year.</p>
