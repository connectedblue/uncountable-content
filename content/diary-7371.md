<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Only one harvester today and four of us, so all fairly relaxed.  There was really not much grass coming off at all, so it was a high quality, species rich mix right from the off.  We had a visit from the land owner mid morning and it was very interesting to hear how they manage the land with the beef cattle.  They are due on this field in the autumn.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We finished up around 2pm because Tom had to get back to the barns.  But the haul was still substantial &#8211; I&#8217;d guess around 50kg.</p>
