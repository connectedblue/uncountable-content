<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A slightly delayed monthly visit to the Swimming Spot with Graham for the riverfly check.  In previous months we&#8217;d seen both low scores and counts, but last month we started to see <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2026/09/mayfly-in-september/" data-type="post" data-id="7475">signs of improvement</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We very much built on that today.  For a start, four bullhead fish were caught in the net which we photographed and put straight back.  These will get recorded in iRecord and increase the county fish records.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We then proceeded to scoop out numerous mayfly, as we did last month.  But also tons of olives and cased caddis.  In the end, a score of 9, with three species having a score a 2 and the other a 3. This feels like a solid path back to abundance which we&#8217;d hope to observe next month.</p>
