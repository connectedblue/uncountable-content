<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Met up with Graham in the morning for our regular riverfly check on the Frome near the Ocean.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The first thing we noticed in the bucket was a complete abundance of water beetles.  Usually we see a few, but they were literally everywhere.  And, in stark contrast, not a lot of gammarus on immediate looking.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We settled down to do the samples and had to look very hard to find something.  A few cased caddis and olives showed themselves after a while.  But we could hardly see any gammarus.  A count of 11 in the end, which is extraordinarily poor.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I don&#8217;t think it&#8217;s pollution &#8211; it&#8217;s just the poor habitat caused by the low river levels and flow.  We didn&#8217;t have a technical breach.  We managed a score of 5, but this was a case of the score flattering the actual counts.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Let&#8217;s hope next month we see an improvement.</p>
