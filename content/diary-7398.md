<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Headed over towards Stroud Brewery with <a href="https://www.stroudvalleysproject.org/">Stroud Valleys Project</a> to deal with the Himalayan Balsam on a new stretch of canal.  We started off on the opposite side to the tow path where it was pretty dense.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There were quite a few of us, and we managed to move quite methodically through the area.  Although it hasn&#8217;t been pulled by our group in the last few years, it hadn&#8217;t completely taken over.  The native vegetation was still holding it&#8217;s own.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By break time we had completely finished that side, which was very satisfying.  We couldn&#8217;t reach parts right down by the water in front of dense trees &#8211; they would need to be done by boat.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We could then start on the towpath side, which apart from the first part was very clear &#8211; a testament to work done in previous years along here.  So then we went down to Bagpath bridge and tackled the other side where quite a bit had sprung up, particularly where construction tracks have been laid.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Again, with quite a few of us, we managed to methodically go through this whole area before lunch.  So all in all, a successful first sweep of this part of the canal and will be interesting to see how it fares next year. </p>
