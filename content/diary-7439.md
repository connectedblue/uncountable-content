<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Returned to the cricket club with <a href="https://www.stroudvalleysproject.org/">Stroud Valleys Project</a> for the last Himalayan Balsam pulling of the season.  There were a few different routes we could take through the woods to known areas of Balsam from previous years.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Up until tea break it was a bit bitty with just small amounts spread over a wide area.  Everything is in flower now, but surprisingly most of the flower heads were not popping seed when pulled, which makes it worthwhile doing.  After tea break we found a very substantial, densely populated along a fairly long stretch of river bank.</p>
