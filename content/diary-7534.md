<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Today&#8217;s seeding site with <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a> was a return to a site <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2023/10/sowing-seed-in-scrubditch/" data-type="post" data-id="2541">we did back in 2023</a>.  Unfortunately, due to a variety of issues the seed sown back then did  not take at all.  A number of changes were made to the site and so today we were putting a second lot down to get it going again.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Last time I was seeding from the back of the landrover, but today we had enough people that seeding by foot was possible.  Two fields totally 11 hectares, with the morning one slightly smaller.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Although it had been lashing down all the way on the drive over, once we got onsite, the clouds cleared and it was really warm.  No jacket needed.  The first field had been done by a smaller team last week who had very wisely left clear markers where they had got to.  So after the usual alignment, we quickly got a long line traversing the field.  By mid morning break, we had comfortably finished that meadow.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Line discipline on the second field turned out to be more problematic.  Although it seemed as if we should simply follow the grass lines, in practice we drifted either too far apart or too close together.  After a couple of lines we settled into a kind of diagonal pattern led out by the first marker.  Even then, drift occurred, and everyone just did their own thing, blaming the field instead.  But as usual, we got all the seed spread evenly across the field with none left over.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Anne joined us today and, as usual, I had fascinating and very helpful conversations with her about the Evenlode catchment partnership.  It was very timely as some of the discussions we are having in the Severn Vale partnership about citizen science have already been tackled to a good extent in their project.  So there&#8217;s a lot we should probably learn.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Also had a good conversation with Sam who has used different apps for field survey collection,  wider than just grasslands.  It&#8217;s always good to hear how ecologists do these processes as it all helps to feed in to the data pipeline analysis methodologies which I&#8217;m trying to progress.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We finished by lunchtime and I was home by 2pm.  </p>
