<p class="wp-block-paragraph">My second time hosting the <a href="https://indieweb.org/IndieWeb_Carnival">IndieWeb blog carnival</a> , which this month was on the subject of <a href="/august-2026-indieweb-carnival-purpose" data-type="post" data-id="2360">Purpose</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There were 23 submissions, including at least three bloggers who entered the carnival for the first time.  I&#8217;m so happy they chose my month to start, and I look forward to reading their posts in future carnivals.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Of course, August is also <a href="https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/">Blaugust</a>, so quite a few entries were also part of that challenge.  It&#8217;s always a joy to see the blogging community thrive.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There were as many different takes on Purpose as there were entries.  I found myself challenging my own perspective as I read the submissions.  I&#8217;m really not so convinced as I was that a sense of purpose is a pre-requisite for a fulfilling life.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Without further ado, here are all the submissions along with my favourite quotes from their post:</p>

<h2><a href="https://nullish.smol.pub/purpose-intention-action">Purpose, intention, action</a></h2>
<div>By Nullish</div>
</header>
<p>Internally, purpose is ours alone to define. Purpose can give shape to intention, which in turn can drive direct and specific action. My purpose in life is to feel connected to others. The fulfillment of purpose starts with my intentions: community engagement, participation, an exchange of ideas. Intention is a basis for specific actions or behaviors: I attend a community townhall, an event at my child&#8217;s school, or participate in an indieweb carnival.<br><br>

Action without intention is random or impulsive. Intention without purpose may be misdirected or unsatisfying. Purpose unsupported it is little more than the foreshadowing of regret.</p>
</article>
