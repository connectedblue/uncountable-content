<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The second day of the <a href="https://www.tyndalearts.co.uk/">Tyndale Arts Trail</a>, so we decided to visit all the venues and look at the exhibits.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">First off was Leaf &amp; Ground, which was slightly more convoluted to get to since the main road had been closed unexpectedly.  But it was a good start, with some interesting furniture art in among the paintings.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We then headed back to Kingshill house which is one of the main two venues.  Pretty much all the rooms were full of exhibits so a lot to look at.  Most of the artists were also there, so a good opportunity to ask about their work.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After that, it was the centre of Dursley for the Castle bar and Methodist church displays.  Also a good opportunity to have tea and cake.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The final venue was Prema in Uley which was the best display.  It was a single artist and mostly consisted of local scenes, both natural and industrial.  The artist was on hand to talk through his work.  It&#8217;s something he does on the side rather than trying to sell the work, but he had in fact sold a couple of nice pieces.</p>
