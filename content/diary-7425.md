<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I saw the total eclipse in 1999 down at Lake Balaton in Hungary.  Today was the closest we had in the UK since then, and we had about 93% totality in Gloucestershire.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Went up to Uley Bury to view it.  I had a home made eclipse viewer, as well as a kitchen colander.  It was an extremely warm evening and there were a lot of people sitting on the bury edge waiting for 7.15pm.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At the peak time, the light definitely dimmed for a minute or two, but nowhere near darkness.  My homemade equipment wasn&#8217;t very good really, but someone up there let me look through his glasses which was very impressive &#8211; just a sliver of sun showing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">My whatsapp groups were full of pictures after.  It seems that there was traffic congestion at all the main grassland hills around Stroud, so definitely picked the right place to view.</p>
