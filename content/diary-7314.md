<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I bumped into Richard and we ended up having a long conversation about community, politics and AI.  So we decided to head over to the methodist hall for lunch and continue the conversation there.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By the time we&#8217;d finished it was 2pm and time to find Leah to get ready for the <a href="https://discoverdursley.org.uk/shop-local-2026/">Shop Local</a> prize draw.  We started planning this scheme last year, and this was the first major draw of the year.  There were hundreds of entries, which is an excellent response from the community.  I got handed the mic to say some impromptu words about the scheme and then compered as the Deputy Mayor carried out the draw.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Afterwards, I headed back to Uley for the second community event of the day.  The <a href="https://www.cucan.org.uk/Uley-trees-and-meadows">Uley Trees &amp; Meadows</a> were holding a fund raiser which consisted of a self guided nature walk around the village and ending up in the walled garden for scones and tea.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The rather brilliant addition to the walk was to have members of the group stationed at different points on the route to explain more about the biodiversity in that part.  It was such a great way to engage the community and raise awareness of different things we can all do to increase wildlife benefit in our own spaces.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I particularly enjoyed seeing the gardens of Matt and Anna and also Debs and Ian.  There really is an awful lot of good stuff going on in these private places you don&#8217;t normally get to see.</p>
