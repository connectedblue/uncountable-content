<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Very busy day ahead today, and kicked off by meeting with Tony at Stroud for the monthly riverfly check at Ebley Meadows.  We selected the upper site again this month because it&#8217;s still accessible and there was more chance of finding a diverse set of species.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Although river levels were still low, the bucket looked promising at first glance. We rescued a large bullhead and then set about analysing the sample. As it turned out, the score was middling &#8211; plenty of gammarus, but no count of over 10 for other species.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I then headed over to Sapperton Wilder to join the <a href="https://www.cotswolds-nl.org.uk/looking-after/our-grasslands-projects/glorious-cotswolds-grasslands/">Glorious Grasslands</a> team on the first seeding spread of the year.  I&#8217;ve never actually been here before, despite several of my local projects doing various activities over the years.  Today&#8217;s task was the seeding of a couple of fields which would use up 250kg of this year&#8217;s haul.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I arrived a bit late, but the discipline in force was plain to see.  Lines of people with buckets moving together across the field and stopping in unison to top up at the various bag points.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Spent a pleasant couple of hours walking up and down chucking seed. I had to leave at 12.30 though to get home and changed for my afternoon appointments.  I was gutted to see later in the WhatsApp group that Anna had found a barn owl in one of the estate boxes and ringed the breeding female.  Over the last seven years I&#8217;ve hardly  ever left a GCG task early, and the one day I do, I miss out on seeing an owl.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Next up was the <a href="https://www.chantrycentre.org/">Chantry Centre</a> for a meeting of the Thrive Local project.  We&#8217;re pretty much there on the print booklet of events for Autumn which will go off to be printed shortly.  We had a pretty good discussion on the best way to market the guide once it launches.  There are some key local events we could piggy back off, as well as the usual facebook page and press releases and so on.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Final port of call for the day was the library for the first <a href="https://www.facebook.com/dursleycodeclub">Dursley Code Club</a> of the new school year.  It was good to see a couple of boys in their Rednock uniforms for the first time as they enter year 7.  We only had four today, but it felt extremely busy as both Trevor and I were rushing around trying to help them remember where they got to with their projects.</p>
