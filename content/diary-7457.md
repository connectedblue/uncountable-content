<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After the break, Jamie took the wheel while Tom and I looked after the tarp.  We were visited by Sophia, the land manager, and it was interesting to hear what further management she could do to make the field even more species rich next season.  The beef cattle will be coming on in the autumn, so it will get a good graze.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Just two bags by the end of the day, but it contained some quality stuff which could perhaps be mixed in with other batches during the allocation process.  Last actual harvest will be tomorrow, then the barn work begins to sort out where all this seed will be distrubuted. </p>
