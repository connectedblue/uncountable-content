<p class="wp-block-paragraph">What with one thing and another over the last month, I didn&#8217;t get a chance to book on any of the walks in this year&#8217;s <a href="https://dursleywelcomeswalkers.org.uk/">Dursley Walking Festival</a>. So I was very happy to join Helen and Nigel for a short walk this morning through the valleys of Lasborough and Bagpath.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A good crowd turned out at the carpark at Hunter&#8217;s Hall.  Among them was Andy, which meant we rattled on about <a href="https://discoverdursley.org.uk/">Discover Dursley</a> for the first 30 minutes or so, completely missing the landscape we walked through.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Helen and Nigel were great at giving extra information at points of interest.  This is a route I have <a href="https://diary.uncountable.uk/2024/10/walking-through-hidden-valleys/" data-type="post" data-id="3727">done many times</a>, but I didn&#8217;t know some of the history of the mounds on the route.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We also stopped by the church in Lasborough which is a fabulous remote spot and got an update on the new occupants of the manor.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The interesting thing in Bagpath which I hadn&#8217;t seen before is that the crumbling Norman church has been privately bought and is being restored as a residence.  It&#8217;s not finished yet, but clearly it&#8217;s going to be an impressive structure.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All in all, a nice jaunt round in the fresh autumn air.</p>
